The Tina Project

With HomeAid Austin & Henley Homes

Answering the Call, and Being Answered.

For 23 years, Tina Pesina was the calm voice on the other end of the line, an APD 911 dispatcher guiding others through their most difficult moments. Now she is facing one of her own, and it is our chance to show up for her.

After losing her leg at 17, Tina refused to let it define her story. She became a competitive wheelchair racer, built a meaningful career with APD, and created a life rooted in strength and perseverance. But today, the place that should support her most, her home, no longer meets her needs.

Freccia is proud to stand alongside HomeAid Austin, Operation Finally Home, and Gary Henley of Henley Homes to help change that: a home renovated for accessibility, dignity, and independence. This is about a fresh start, and helping write her next chapter.

You can follow the project and support it on Freccia's Instagram.
