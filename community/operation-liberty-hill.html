Operation Liberty Hill · Liberty Hill, TX

When the Community Shows Up.

The Cajun Navy delivered a huge truckload of clothes, cleaning supplies, hygiene products, and more, all donated to Operation Liberty Hill. An incredible act of generosity in support of our community.

Local police, fire, and Celebration Church showed up to help unload and lend a hand. It was a beautiful event to witness and be part of, and a reminder of what neighbors can do when they come together.

Together, we make a difference.
