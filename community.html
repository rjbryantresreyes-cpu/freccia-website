The Work

Giving Back, Year After Year.

Giving back is a core part of who we are at Freccia. These are some of the community projects and causes we have been honored to be part of.

Central Texas SPCA · Leander

A New Home for Tails of Hope

We are proud to build the new Central Texas SPCA shelter, now known as Tails of Hope, a safe and welcoming place for animals waiting on their forever homes. Ten years in the making, and exactly the kind of work we want more of.

Read the Story: A New Home for Tails of Hope

Peace Lutheran & HomeAid Austin

Keeping Families Together

In Austin, shelters are often split into men's and women's, which forces families apart at their most vulnerable moments. The Peace Family Housing Project changes that, a transitional home where families stay together while working toward stable, long-term housing.

Read the Story: Keeping Families Together

HomeAid Austin & Henley Homes

The Tina Project

Tina Pesina spent 23 years as an APD 911 dispatcher, the calm voice guiding others through their hardest moments. We are honored to help renovate her home for accessibility and independence, alongside HomeAid Austin and Gary Henley of Henley Homes.

Read the Story: The Tina Project

Ongoing Partnership

Partners with HomeAid Austin

Our co-owner Rebekah Severi serves on HomeAid Austin's Fund Development Committee, and our team shows up for serve days across the city, from building care kits for local nonprofits to public care days. Showing up, not just building.

Read the Story: Partners with HomeAid Austin

Operation Liberty Hill

Neighbors Helping Neighbors

When a truckload of donated clothes, cleaning supplies, and hygiene products arrived for Operation Liberty Hill, local police, fire, and neighbors showed up to help unload. A reminder of what a community can do when it comes together.

Read the Story: Neighbors Helping Neighbors

Sunrise Navigation Center

Showing Up for Sunrise

Sunrise Navigation Center works with people experiencing homelessness in Austin. We helped them with a remodel of their facilities, then volunteered alongside HomeAid Austin and Braeburn on a care kit project Sunrise benefited from. Two ways to show up for the same people.

Read the Story: Showing Up for Sunrise
