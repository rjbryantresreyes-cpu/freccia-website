Commercial FAQ

Questions Property Owners
Actually Ask Us.

Most tenant improvements in Austin Metro range from 6 to 20 weeks depending on scope, permit timelines, and material lead times. A simple office build-out typically runs 8-12 weeks from permit approval. Restaurant and retail TIs with heavy MEP work can run 16-20 weeks. We provide a detailed project schedule at the start of every engagement.
Commercial build-outs in Austin Metro typically range from $50 to $200+ per square foot depending on finish level, trade requirements, and existing conditions. Office spaces typically run $60-$120/sqft. Restaurant and retail spaces can run $100-$200/sqft due to kitchen, plumbing, and specialty finish requirements. Exact costs depend heavily on the existing shell condition and finish specifications.
Yes, any permitted commercial construction work in Texas requires a licensed general contractor. A GC coordinates all trades, manages the permit process, and ensures work meets code. Attempting to self-manage subcontractors on a commercial TI typically results in coordination problems, liability exposure, and permit issues that cost more to fix than a GC would have cost to hire.
In a design-build contract, a single firm handles both architectural design and construction, which reduces coordination gaps, compresses the timeline, and gives you one point of accountability. It works best for commercial projects where speed and budget certainty matter more than having an independent architect check the GC. Freccia offers design-build services through our network of architectural and engineering partners.
Look for: a valid Texas contractor license, commercial-specific experience (not just residential), references from similar project types, a track record of on-time delivery, and a team you can communicate with clearly. Ask for a project list with owner contacts you can call. Be cautious of the lowest bid: in commercial construction, budget certainty and schedule adherence matter more than winning on price.
Our pre-construction scope includes a detailed budget estimate, full scope document, preliminary project schedule, permit submittal strategy, and an introduction to all major trade partners. We document every decision made in pre-construction so there are no surprises once we break ground. This phase typically takes 2-4 weeks and is the most important investment in a successful project.
An office build-out typically refers to the construction of a new office space within a raw shell, while an office renovation involves updating or modifying an existing office space. Both require permitting and code compliance, but a build-out usually involves more ground-up MEP work while a renovation works around existing systems.
We begin with a free consultation to discuss your build-out or tenant improvement needs. From there, we develop a project plan and budget tailored to your specific requirements. Once you approve the plan, our team handles all aspects of the construction process, keeping you informed throughout the project.
Residential FAQ

Custom Home and Renovation
Questions Answered.

A full kitchen renovation in Austin Metro typically takes 6-12 weeks from demolition to final punch list, depending on scope. Mid-level renovations with standard cabinetry and countertop selections typically land at 8 weeks. Fully custom kitchens with specialty cabinetry and stone work can run 12-16 weeks due to fabrication and lead times. Planning ahead on selections is the single biggest factor in keeping a kitchen on schedule.
Primary bathroom renovations in Austin Metro typically range from $25,000 to $80,000+ depending on size, scope, and finish level. A mid-level remodel with tile, new fixtures, and vanity replacement typically runs $25,000-$45,000. Full gut-renovations with heated floors, custom tilework, and freestanding tubs can run $60,000-$100,000+. We provide detailed line-item estimates at the start of every project.
Yes, any addition that increases the footprint or square footage of your home requires a building permit in Round Rock and throughout Austin Metro. This includes room additions, garage conversions, ADUs, and screened patios with foundation work. Freccia handles the full permit process, from plan preparation to final inspection. Never skip the permit. Unpermitted additions can create significant problems when you sell or insure the property.
Custom pools in Austin Metro typically range from $65,000 to $150,000+ depending on size, shape, materials, and features. A standard plaster pool with basic decking runs $65,000-$90,000. Pools with tanning ledges, water features, custom tile, travertine decking, and outdoor kitchens can run $120,000-$200,000+. Most outdoor living projects take 10-16 weeks from permit to final walkthrough.
While construction can be invasive, we try to mitigate sound and debris as much as possible, but it will be disruptive. We highly suggest moving out of the home temporarily during construction if feasible, especially for full renovations or additions that affect main living areas.
You can expect some interruption in utilities depending on the scope of your project. Your project manager will let you know about any planned interruptions ahead of time so you can plan around them.
