Built for Retail Spaces

Retail Spaces That Open Strong.

Retail construction runs on a fixed clock: a grand opening date that can't move. Freccia Construction has delivered retail build-outs for national tenants and independent boutique brands alike, coordinating landlord requirements, brand standards, and occupied-center logistics so retail spaces open on time and look the part from day one.

Our retail portfolio spans salon build-outs with custom millwork and lighting, retail and dining plazas anchored by growing local brands, and national tenant improvements with strict finish-out specifications.

Retail Tenant Improvements

Shell-to-finish build-outs for national and local retail tenants, coordinated with landlord base-building work.

Salon & Boutique Build-Outs

Custom millwork, lighting, and finish work for salons and specialty retail brands.

Retail & Dining Plazas

Ground-up and finish-out construction for multi-tenant retail and dining developments.
