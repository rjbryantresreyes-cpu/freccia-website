A Commercial Fitness Facility Built for Performance.

Rebelz Gym needed more than a construction crew. They needed a partner who understood that a commercial fitness facility isn't just four walls and a floor. It's a purpose-built training environment where every square foot has a job to do.

Freccia Construction managed the full project scope: structural build-out, high-load flooring systems rated for commercial gym equipment, specialized HVAC for air quality and temperature management during peak-use hours, electrical systems sized for commercial cardio and weight equipment throughout, and custom locker room construction with commercial plumbing.

What Made This Project Different

Commercial fitness construction has specific code requirements around floor load capacity, ventilation rates, and exit routing that most general contractors don't have consistent experience with. Freccia's team managed the full permit process with Austin city inspectors, coordinated all specialty sub-contractors on schedule, and delivered a finished facility the operator could open on their target date.

The Freccia Approach

One point of contact from groundbreak through certificate of occupancy. Weekly progress reporting. Proactive communication when schedule or scope needed adjustment, not surprises at the end. The operator of Rebelz Gym knew the status of their project at every stage because we built that transparency into how we work.

Since opening, Rebelz Cheer & Tumbling has gone on to become an NCA National Champion program, training and competing in the space Freccia built to their exact specifications.

If you're planning a commercial fitness facility, gym build-out, or any commercial new build in the Austin Metro area, reach out to our team. We'll give you an honest assessment of timeline, cost, and what it actually takes to build it right.
