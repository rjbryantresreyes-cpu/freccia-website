A retail tenant improvement delivered right.

James Avery Plaza is a retail shopping center in the Austin Metro area where Freccia Construction managed a full tenant improvement scope — taking existing commercial shell space and building it out to meet the specific needs of a national retail tenant with high brand standards and tight delivery requirements.

Retail tenant improvements are demanding projects. National tenants have strict finish-out specifications, franchise standards, and grand opening dates that can't move. Landlords need work done with minimal disruption to adjacent tenants. Freccia's team coordinated all of this: managing the TI process from permit through punch list while keeping the project on the timeline that the retail operator needed to open on time.

What tenant improvement work demands

A successful retail TI requires more than building to plan — it requires coordination with the landlord's base building, active adjacent tenants, the retail brand's own project manager, and the local inspection authority, all on a compressed timeline. Freccia managed all four tracks simultaneously, delivering a finished retail environment that met both the tenant's brand standards and the owner's lease requirements.

Austin's commercial retail market

Austin Metro's retail corridor continues to grow across the 183/US-290 corridors, Round Rock, Cedar Park, and Georgetown. Freccia has built and improved commercial retail space throughout this market since 2016. We know the inspectors, the subcontractors who perform at the quality retail tenants expect, and the logistics of working in occupied shopping centers without impacting neighboring businesses.
