On the Ground in Austin Metro TX

Freccia Construction's field team operates across commercial and residential job sites in Austin Metro, including Round Rock, Cedar Park, Georgetown, Leander, and Austin TX. Edson Rodriguez is part of the team that keeps those sites running every day: coordinating with trade contractors, tracking progress against schedule, flagging issues early, and maintaining the quality standard Freccia has held since 2016.

In the Austin Metro construction market, where skilled labor is in high demand and project timelines are under constant pressure, consistent field execution separates contractors that deliver from those that disappoint. Freccia's field team, and Edson's role in it, is central to the delivery model that keeps clients coming back.
