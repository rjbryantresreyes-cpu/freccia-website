Waldemar, a native of Sugar Land, Texas, was introduced to the real estate and construction industry at a young age. Growing up with a real estate investor father and a property manager mother, Waldemar developed a foundational understanding of property investment and management.

In his professional life in Houston, Waldemar owned and operated three companies within the construction industry before partnering with Rebekah Severi. Together, they bring over 38 years of experience in designing, building, and real estate investment. Their shared expertise led to the founding of Freccia Group in 2016, where they have built a reputation for high-quality work and exceptional client satisfaction.

Word-of-mouth referrals have been instrumental in establishing Freccia Group's strong community presence, a testament to Waldemar and Rebekah's dedication to excellence and personalized service.

As Owner and Managing Partner, Waldemar leads Freccia Construction's work as a licensed general contractor and custom home builder serving Round Rock, Austin, Cedar Park, and Georgetown TX, overseeing both commercial construction and residential renovation projects across Central Texas.

Building Freccia from the Ground Up in Austin TX

Freccia Construction, "freccia" means arrow in Italian, was founded on the principle that construction should move with precision and purpose. Waldemar Severi built the company with a clear direction: deliver commercial and residential construction in Austin Metro that meets a higher standard of craft and communication.

Based in Round Rock, TX, Freccia serves commercial property owners, developers, and homeowners across Austin, Round Rock, Cedar Park, Georgetown, and Leander. The company is licensed in Texas, HUB-certified, BBB-accredited, and holds memberships in the HBA Greater Austin, NARI, NAHB, and AGC.

What Waldemar Believes About Construction

Waldemar's approach to building is straightforward: do the work right, keep the client informed, and never compromise on quality because of schedule pressure. These principles define how every Freccia project is managed, from the first discovery call to the final walkthrough.

That philosophy is reflected in Freccia's track record across commercial tenant improvements, new commercial builds, custom home builds, and residential renovations throughout Austin Metro.

Commercial Construction Leadership in Austin Metro

Under Waldemar's direction, Freccia has delivered notable commercial projects including Rebelz Gym, Building Kidz Daycare at Palmera Ridge (Round Rock TX), and James Avery Plaza, alongside a growing portfolio of office build-outs, restaurant TIs, and commercial remodels across Austin Metro.

Freccia's commercial work spans tenant improvements, design-build contracts, new commercial construction, and property owner services, all coordinated from the Round Rock, TX headquarters.
