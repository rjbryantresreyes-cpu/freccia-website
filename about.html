About Freccia

Built on craft.
Driven by purpose.

Freccia Construction is a family-led builder based in Round Rock, TX. Custom homes and commercial construction across Austin Metro since 2016.

Our Mission

We exist to do more for our community. We hope to give away free homes one day, or do projects for free for people in need.

— Rebekah Severi, Co-Owner, Freccia Construction

A Central Texas builder with something to prove.

Freccia Construction was founded by Rebekah and Waldemar Severi in 2016. What started as a residential custom home builder has grown into two entities — Freccia Group LLC for residential, Freccia Group Commercial LLC for commercial — operating across Austin Metro and greater Central Texas.

The name Freccia means "arrow" in Italian. It reflects the precision and direction behind everything we do. We don't take every project. We take the right ones — the ones where the homeowner or business cares about the result as much as we do.

With a $200K minimum, we've built our reputation on projects that deserve real attention: custom homes where the details matter, commercial spaces where quality and timeline both count, and remodels that transform how people live and work.

What We Stand For

How we build.

Craftsmanship over volume

We don't chase project count. We focus on doing fewer projects better. That's why we have a $200K floor and why we decline work that doesn't fit what we're built for.

Honest communication

You'll know where your project stands. We don't hide behind scheduling or vague updates. When something changes, you hear it from us first.

Design-forward thinking

We care about how things look, not just how they're built. The projects that excite us are the ones where design and construction both have to be right.

Community roots

We've built in Austin Metro for over a decade. We know the contractors, the codes, the neighborhoods. And we genuinely intend to give back — free builds for people in need is a real goal, not a tagline.
