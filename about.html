About Freccia

Built on Craft.
Driven by Purpose.

Freccia Construction is a local, family-owned builder based in Round Rock, TX. Custom homes and commercial construction across Austin Metro since 2016.

Our Mission

We exist to do more for our community: to build spaces that elevate lives and bring people together, with intention, craftsmanship, and unwavering care.

Rebekah Severi, Co-Owner, Freccia Construction

A Central Texas Builder with Something to Prove.

Freccia Construction is led by partners Rebekah Severi, Waldemar Severi, Josh Clements, and Jacqueline Clements. What started in 2016 as a residential custom home builder has grown into two divisions, residential and commercial, operating across Austin Metro and greater Central Texas.

The name Freccia means "arrow" in Italian. It reflects the precision and direction behind everything we do. We don't take every project. We take the right ones, the ones where the homeowner or business cares about the result as much as we do.

We've built our reputation on projects that deserve real attention: custom homes where the details matter, commercial spaces where quality and timeline both count, and remodels that transform how people live and work.

What We Stand For

How We Build.

Quality Over Quantity

We don't chase project count. We focus on doing fewer projects better, and we decline work that doesn't fit what we're built for.

Honest Communication

You'll know where your project stands. We don't hide behind scheduling or vague updates. When something changes, you hear it from us first.

Design-Forward Thinking

We care about how things look, not just how they're built. The projects that excite us are the ones where design and construction both have to be right.

Community Roots

We've built in Austin Metro for over a decade. We know the contractors, the codes, the neighborhoods. This is home, and we build like it.
