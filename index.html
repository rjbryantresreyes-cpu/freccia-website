Our Process

From First Call
To Final Walkthrough.

01

Discovery Call

We start with a conversation. No pitch, no pressure. Just clarity on your vision, timeline, and budget, and whether we're the right fit for your project in Austin Metro.

02

Scope & Design

We build a complete scope with your design team or ours. Every detail is documented before a nail is driven, so there are no surprises mid-build and no budget ambiguity.

03

Build Phase

One point of contact. Weekly updates. A dedicated superintendent on-site every day. You stay fully informed without managing the details yourself.

04

Final Walkthrough

We don't call a project done until you're satisfied. Our punch list process and final walkthrough ensure every detail meets the standard we promised from day one.
